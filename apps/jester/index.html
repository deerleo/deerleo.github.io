How to play

  1. ?Pick a clip.In Blind, nobody sees it. In Normal, you dance along with it.
  2. Pass the laptop.Each player gets a secret part from the clip: one of the people in it.
  3. Copy the shape.Stand back so your whole body is in view. Get inside the shape and move when it moves, like a mirror.
  4. See what you did.Everyone is cut out into the clip, then the best jester gets the spotlight.

Not ready when your turn starts? You can start over once, in the first 5 seconds.

What Jester saves is in Settings.

Settings

What Jester saves

  • Each round's moves are saved as points on a body over time, with the score and timing. Never video, never a picture of you. With each round we also save a random id for this browser, the browser and graphics chip it runs on, your camera's size and frame rate, and which clip you played, so we can tell a slow machine from unfair scoring. The team uses all this to test and tune the scoring.
  • A clip you send in is kept private until it's reviewed. To check it, a few frames, what the game measured, its name and your name go to Anthropic, the company that makes the AI.
  • In an online room, your photo goes to everyone in the room, including people who join after you, until you leave. A short cut-out of you goes to the others in your round for the recap and the crowning. Neither is stored.
  • Your settings and how many of you play stay in this browser.
