How to play

The board has 4 rings and 8 sectors — 32 cells. Players take turns placing a mark (X goes first) on any empty cell. The first to get four in a row wins. If the board fills up with no winner, it’s a draw.

Tips

  • Three in a row with the fourth cell empty is a threat — your opponent must block it.
  • Make two threats at once and they can only block one.
  • Every cell sits on 7 patterns. Rings wrap around, so there are no “edges” sideways.
  • Stuck? Hint shows the best move for the player whose turn it is.

Controls

  • Click / tap a cell to play. Keyboard: Tab or arrow keys to move, Enter to place.
  • Ctrl+Z undo   Ctrl+Y redo
  • Alt+H hint   Alt+N new game   Alt+M mute

Can Master be beaten?

Only if you move first. This game is solved: the first player can always force a win by opening on the innermost (or outermost) ring. Master knows that win — when it plays X it wins every single game, guaranteed (checked against every possible defence). When you play X, a winning line exists for you, but you have to find it: one slip and Master takes over.
