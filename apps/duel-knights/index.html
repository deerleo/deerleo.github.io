How to fight

First to three rounds
W A S DMove — you face where the mouse aims Left clickLight attack — swords, the spear and the axe chain three blows, the mace two Right clickHeavy attack — slow, crushes guards. Tap Space mid-windup to feint SpaceBlock. Raise it just before the blow lands to parry ShiftDodge toward your movement keys QFocus (lock facing onto your foe) P · MPause · mute
Every action costs stamina — an empty bar breaks your guard. Weapons only wound what they actually touch.
A mace drains your guard fast and its blows bleed through the shield: dodge it, or parry it.
A spear thrusts from beyond sword reach, so step inside the point. When its head starts to glow, the long thrust is coming. An axe hits hardest of all, but a raised shield shrugs it off.
